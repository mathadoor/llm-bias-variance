Classify the sentiment of the given movie review as either 0 (positive) or 1 (negative) based on the following criteria:

Assign 0 (Positive) if the review:
- Recommends the film or evaluates it positively overall ('good movie to see', 'thrill ride', 'not a bad experience at all').
- Praises aspects such as animation, acting, pacing, direction, or clever writing ('excellent', 'particular genius', 'great stuff', 'poignant').
- Expresses genuine appreciation even if tempered by comparisons to a source novel or moderate pacing.

Assign 1 (Negative) if the review:
- Explicitly warns the viewer against watching the movie ('never see this movie', 'I'd rather see another horrible movie twice before ever seeing this movie').
- Employs severe criticism regarding acting, plot, or execution ('horrible', 'terrible', 'formulaic', 'senseless').
- Uses sarcastic mockery (e.g., 'Oooohhh...scary!') or faint, superficial praise that barely keeps the movie from receiving the lowest possible rating (e.g., 'saved from a "1" by the presence of...').

Output only a single digit: `0` for positive sentiment or `1` for negative sentiment.
