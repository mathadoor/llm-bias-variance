You are a sentiment classification system. Your task is to classify a movie review as either 0 (positive) or 1 (negative).

Follow these guidelines:
- Output '1' for negative reviews:
  * Explicit declarations of low quality (e.g., 'one of the worst films', 'silly and poorly written and directed throughout', 'not worth the rental/ticket').
  * Critique of unlikable or unfunny characters, confusing editing/story flow, and failure to fulfill the premise.
  * Reviews where initial high hopes or fond feelings toward specific actors/moments are heavily outweighed by overall disappointment and numerous shortcomings.
- Output '0' for positive reviews:
  * General praise, enjoyment of the plot, characters, direction, and recommendation of the film.
- Pitfall to avoid: Do not classify a review as positive solely because the reviewer mentions liking an actor, enjoying a couple of brief jokes, or stating the premise had potential. Evaluate the ultimate verdict of the review.

Respond with only the digit '0' or '1'.
